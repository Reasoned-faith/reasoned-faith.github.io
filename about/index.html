About Reasoned Faith

Hello, I’m {{ site.author }}. This site is where I put in writing what I have studied about the Christian faith, and how it answers the hard questions people ask.

Why this site exists

Questions about God, Jesus, the Bible and other religions deserve serious answers, not slogans. Reasoned Faith exists to give those answers in writing: carefully researched, clearly explained, and open to checking.

I believe that faith and careful thinking belong together. The verse that guides this work is 1 Peter 3:15: be ready to give a reason for the hope that is in you, “with meekness and fear.” That means answering honestly, with evidence, and with respect for the person asking.

What you will find here

Long, carefully sourced articles on questions such as:

  • Who Jesus claimed to be, and what the first Christians believed about him.
  • The Trinity, the incarnation and the biblical teaching about God.
  • What the early chapters of Genesis say, and how they relate to history and archaeology.
  • How the Quran and the Islamic tradition compare with the Bible, based on the Islamic sources themselves.
  • How Hindu traditions look when read next to the biblical account.

How I try to write

I try to quote the sources and give references, so that you do not have to take my word for anything. I try to state other views the way their own followers would state them. If an argument is weak, I say so, even when Christians often use it. And if I get something wrong, I want to know so that I can correct it.

A word to readers of other faiths

Some of these subjects are sensitive. I criticise ideas and sources, not people. You are welcome here, and you are welcome to disagree and tell me where you think I am mistaken.
