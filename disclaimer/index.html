Disclaimer and Privacy

A short, plain statement about how to read this site and how it treats your information.

About the content

The articles on {{ site.title }} are the research and opinions of {{ site.author }}. They are published for education and discussion. They do not speak for any church, denomination, ministry or organisation.

Some articles examine the beliefs and sources of other religions. The aim is to evaluate ideas and texts, not to insult or attack people. Quotations and references are given so that readers can check them, and corrections are welcome.

Accuracy and corrections

Every effort is made to quote sources accurately, but mistakes can happen. If you find an error, please report it through the contact page.

Privacy

  • This site has no user accounts, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not run analytics.
  • Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to the site.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when a page loads.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs under GitHub’s privacy policy.
  • If you message the author through WhatsApp or email, that message is handled by those services and is used only to reply to you.

External links and sharing

Articles link to outside sources, and the share buttons open third-party services such as WhatsApp, Facebook and X. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this site is not responsible for their content.

Copyright

Articles may be quoted briefly with a link back to the original page. Please ask through the contact page before republishing a whole article.
